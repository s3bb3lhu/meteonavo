# Photographic weather assets

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. Illustrative weather cutouts, not real observations or radar imagery. Original alpha retained in a web-optimized 768x768 WebP atlas; sixteen cells mapped to WMO codes and day/night variants. Source PNG kept with local deliverables. Natural photographic lighting replaces sculpted SVG cloud shapes. Freezing precipitation and snow grains share representative precipitation images; they remain correctly labeled.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: ONE production weather sprite atlas, a transparent PNG sheet in an exact 4-column by 4-row grid of 16 equally sized square cells. No labels or grid lines. All subjects fully contained in their own cell, centered with at least 12% transparent padding. Photorealistic atmospheric photography cutouts, real intricate turbulent vapor cloud textures with wispy feathered edges, physically natural lighting. Not icons, not cartoons, not 3D render, not plastic, not rounded sculpted blobs, no vector outlines, no symbols for precipitation. Transparent background, no sky rectangles, no scenery. Row 1 left to right: (1) photographed bright sun disc with subtle natural glare, (2) detailed photographed waxing crescent moon with real lunar craters, (3) small actual cumulus cloud partially covering sun, (4) small wispy actual cloud crossing crescent moon. Row 2: (5) scattered real sunlit cumulus clouds with sun behind, (6) scattered real moonlit clouds with crescent moon behind, (7) dense dark grey natural overcast cloud bank, (8) low soft photographic fog bank with wisps. Row 3: (9) grey raincloud with fine photographed drizzle streaks beneath, (10) dark stormcloud with abundant real rainfall streaks beneath, (11) raincloud with sleety icy droplets beneath, (12) grey snowcloud with natural tiny irregular falling white snow crystals beneath. Row 4: (13) sunlit shower cloud with real falling rain, (14) snow shower cloud with faint sun behind, (15) dark thundercloud with real branching lightning discharge beneath, (16) dark thundercloud with real lightning and small falling hailstones. Maintain consistent photographic realism throughout, atmospheric volumes rather than toy balloons. Do not draw snowflake glyphs or droplet icons. Exact square canvas.
