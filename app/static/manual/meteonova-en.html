METEONOVA · User guide

For private, non-commercial use only. Commercial, business and professional use is not permitted by the project license. Manage, style and play your weather channels.

1. Administration design and colour

  1. Click “Choose design” at the top. The popup offers A · Redline Control, B · Crimson Glass and C · Broadcast Desk.
  2. Choose red, green, blue, royal blue, purple, dark purple or anthracite. The METEONOVA logo follows the selected colour. Changes apply immediately.
  3. Redline displays a compact channel list. Crimson provides large thumbnails. Broadcast offers a large live preview and a design editor with settings alongside it.

2. Day, night and language

  1. Use the top switch to change day/night mode. The flag menu offers German, English, French and Spanish.
  2. Layout, colour, mode and language are saved in this browser and survive navigation. Other browsers and devices keep their own preferences.
  3. Language selection translates administration, sign-in and this guide. Your names, news content and the language of the broadcast weather programme are not automatically translated.

3. Set up a channel and location

  1. Open Settings → Channels & addresses and choose a channel. Search an address, select a result and verify its accuracy. Alternatively, enter both GPS coordinates.
  2. Check country and place name. Enable weather pages and set their order and duration. Enable the channel and click “Save channel”. Changes normally appear within about 15 seconds.

4. Weather design, frames and TV logo

  1. In Design selection, choose the correct channel first. Select a weather template, icons and colours. These settings affect the weather programme, separately from the administration design.
  2. Set top and middle opacity separately: 0 = transparent, 100 = opaque, empty = template. D1–D6 also support custom frame colour, line width and corners.
  3. Upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP logo up to 5 MB. Use PNG or WebP for transparency. Place it left in the location frame or freely, and adjust width and opacity. Click “Save design for this channel”. The preview also displays unsaved values.
  4. “Position logo visually” opens the visual editor. Drag the logo to move it, or its bottom-right handle to resize it. Moving switches it to free positioning. “Apply position” transfers the draft; then save the channel design. “Cancel” discards changes. Arrow keys and Shift help with fine adjustments.

5. Music, radio and news

  1. Open Music / radio. Upload files, create a playlist, add tracks and use arrows to sort them. Assign the playlist to the channel and save its audio source.
  2. Alternatively choose a country, and a state for Germany, select a radio station and save it as the audio source. Audio settings are per channel.
  3. Enter a news RSS URL under Channels & addresses. Official regional weather alerts appear first in the ticker when a matching alert region is available.

6. Radar, country weather, traffic and cameras

  1. Save radar style and distance under Channels & addresses. The map, radar and pin share the same GPS point and view. Satellite imagery is archival; night appearance is a filter.
  2. Select up to eight places for the country map. Names and model temperatures appear directly on it. Enable this page in the programme order.
  3. Enter a TomTom key under Outputs & sources. “Test TomTom API” tests an entered or saved key without automatically saving it. Large areas are split into sections. HTTP 401/403 indicates key/permission issues, HTTP 429 a limit. Zero incidents can still be a successful test.
  4. Select cameras or add an authorised public source under Weather cameras. Enable the camera page in the programme.
  5. Motorways and main/national roads appear before local reports. Each page contains three reports and stays for the configured channel page duration. The programme continues only after all traffic pages. With no reports, a notice appears for ten seconds; source failure is shown separately. The top “Your TV logo” button is available in all three administration layouts.
  6. YouTube cameras: add a custom source under Weather cameras, choose “YouTube video / livestream” and paste a watch, youtu.be or /live/ video link. YouTube URLs are also detected automatically. Enter the camera’s real position manually. The official player is muted in the preview and browser renderer; selected music/radio stay separate. Embedding restrictions, private/removed videos, region, sign-in or bot checks can prevent playback. YouTube files are not extracted and restrictions are not bypassed. Channel and playlist links without a video ID are not accepted.

7. Streams and Xtream

  1. The overview offers visual preview, video playback and an individual M3U. The combined playlist contains enabled channels. The visual preview is silent; audio is included in the video stream.
  2. Create a separate Xtream login under Outputs & sources. In TiviMate use http://SERVER:7373 and those credentials. MPEG-TS and HLS are supported. Refresh the playlist after changes.

8. Account and troubleshooting

  1. Change username and password in My account. Passwords require at least eight characters, an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol. A change ends previous sessions.
  2. No stream: enable the channel, check location, wait briefly and reconnect. No sound: enable player audio and check the saved audio source. No traffic: run the API test and follow its result.
  3. Overview thumbnails come from the existing stream and are cached. The administration layout does not create another video encoder. Redline Control can be a lighter view for slower devices.

9. HTTPS administration and HTTP Xtream

  1. Open administration at https://SERVER:7373. Sign in again once after migration; your username and password remain unchanged. In downloaded guides replace SERVER with your own host.
  2. Xtream remains available at http://SERVER:7373. HTTP administration requests redirect to HTTPS; sign-in and saving require HTTPS.
  3. The Mainhattan camera uses a refreshed JPEG instead of the faulty provider page. Capture time and any stale warning remain visible. Check content rights before public rebroadcast.
